# Source Research

Read `plan.md` and any existing source index. Start from the claims, examples, or assets needed for each affected chapter, including any new questions from authoring or review. Prefer primary sources that match the target versions. Record mismatches and label the version or revision you actually checked.

Apply the [reuse policy](references/reuse-policy.md) before retaining material. Verify the grant for each exact document, snippet, figure, or asset. Check separately credited or licensed material on its own. If an item fails the policy, retain only its metadata and the reason for exclusion, and look for a compatible replacement.

Follow the book's established layout. For a new layout, save approved material under `research/sources/` and keep the source index at `research/index.md`. Preserve required license and attribution notices with the saved material. Update an existing entry when revisiting the same version of a source.

For each approved item, record the title and creator, original URL, version or revision, exact material covered, local path, license and grant evidence, credit and notice requirements, and the claims or chapters it supports. Record exclusions and evidence gaps in the index or existing research notes. Identify the affected claim or chapter and the impact on drafting.

Before handing off, confirm that each approved local path resolves and that the author can trace the evidence and satisfy the reuse conditions. Send gaps that change book scope or prerequisites back to `$books-01-plan-book`. Hand the approved index and any narrower gaps to `$books-03-draft-book` when the requested work continues into drafting.
