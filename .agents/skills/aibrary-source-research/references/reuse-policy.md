# Aibrary source reuse policy

Apply this policy both to copies stored in the book project and to material reproduced or adapted in the manuscript.

| Treatment | License or status | Evidence and handling |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eligible content | Verified public domain, CC0 1.0, CC BY 4.0 | Record the applicable public-domain basis or license grant. Preserve required credits and notices, and record changes when required. |
| Eligible code | MIT, BSD-2-Clause, BSD-3-Clause, Apache-2.0 | Confirm that the grant covers the code being reused. Carry through the applicable license text, copyright notices, credits, and any required change or NOTICE information. |
| Compatibility decision | CC BY-SA, other share-alike terms, and unlisted licenses | Keep the material out of approved sources until the project's redistribution policy permits the intended use and the index documents how its conditions will be met. |
| Exclude | NonCommercial or NoDerivatives variants, all-rights-reserved material, and missing, vague, or conflicting terms | Keep only metadata and a reason for exclusion. Find an eligible substitute or record the gap. |

Locate the terms at the source revision. Record the grant URL and the specific place that establishes its scope, such as the item notice, file header, or applicable directory license. For third-party material embedded in an otherwise licensed source, check that item's grant before including it in a local copy. If the material can be separated, retain only the approved portion and document the omission. When scope or obligations remain unclear, leave the item unapproved.
