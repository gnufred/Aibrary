# What the cold-shower trial counted

> For illness days there was no significant group effect.

From the abstract, Results, third sentence. The authors also reported fewer days of sickness absence from work in the intervention group. Both outcomes were self-reported. Use the sentence together with that context in chapter 4 if an exact quotation would help.
