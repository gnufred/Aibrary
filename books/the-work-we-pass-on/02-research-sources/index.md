## Publication decision

The seven-chapter narrative can proceed using original reporting prose, attributed paraphrase, factual chronology, and links. All five recommended resources can serve as evidence. The manifesto’s full text, private drafts, transcripts, slides, screenshots, and third-party comments each need their own clearance before reproduction. The book’s two exercises can use original material throughout. [Books reuse policy](../../../.agents/skills/books-02-research-sources/references/reuse-policy.md) · [U.S. Copyright Office on facts, ideas, and expression](https://www.copyright.gov/circs/circ33.pdf)

The table distinguishes the rights actually found from clearance under Books’s policy. A site-specific permission can be meaningful even when Books still needs a compatibility decision for its intended use. The policy keeps unlisted terms out of the approved copies until that decision is documented. Direct quotations also raise questions separate from licensing. For example, French law recognizes some justified short quotations with identification of the author and source, and U.S. fair use depends on the circumstances. Neither is the reuse basis recorded for this book. [French Intellectual Property Code, article L122-5](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000006278912/) · [U.S. Copyright Office fair use guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/fair-use/)
